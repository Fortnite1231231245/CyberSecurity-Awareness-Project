Principles you applied

Read domains character by character

Attackers rely on readers skimming. A single substituted character turns the right site into a trap.

Trust the lock, but verify the URL

HTTPS proves the connection is encrypted — not that the destination is legitimate.

Popups asking for SSN are never real

Banks ask through authenticated flows, not overlay forms that anything can inject.

Urgency is a tell
