Core principles

Verify the sender domain

Real banks never use Gmail. Typosquats, extra hyphens, and wrong TLDs are the most common vectors.

Check the URL, not the page

A phishing page can copy every pixel. The address bar is the one thing attackers can't fake.

Urgency is the lure

"Act now," "24 hours," "verify immediately" — all designed to bypass your judgment.

No popup should ever collect SSN

If an overlay asks for sensitive data, close the tab and go to the site directly.
